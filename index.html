Product in Development · Tax Tech

Self Assessment Pro

MTD ITSA-Compliant UK Tax Filing Platform Built with AI Assistance

Product Vision & Market Need

Making Tax Digital for Income Tax Self Assessment (MTD ITSA) is set to reshape UK tax filing, mandating quarterly digital submissions for sole traders and landlords. Built with a co-founder using Antigravity (an AI coding agent), Self Assessment Pro is engineered to eliminate the stress of manual tax calculations, bookkeeping spreadsheets, and complex government forms for micro-businesses.

Current Build Status

Landing page and core UI components are built in Next.js. Currently working through the pre-launch compliance path — AML supervision under MLR2017, KYC and sandbox testing, and finalising terms and conditions — before any live filing capability goes ahead.

Next.js Modern React Architecture
Antigravity AI Coding Agent Workflow
MTD ITSA HMRC Digital Compliance

Pre-Launch Regulatory & Compliance Pathway

  • AML Supervision (MLR2017): Preparing registration for Anti-Money Laundering supervision under the Money Laundering, Terrorist Financing and Transfer of Funds Regulations 2017.
  • KYC & Identity Verification: Designing customer due diligence workflows to verify taxpayer identities and establish audit trails prior to submission.
  • HMRC Developer Sandbox Testing: Connecting with HMRC's Developer Hub sandbox environments to validate quarterly update payloads and end-of-period statements (EOPS).
  • Legal Terms & Conditions: Drafting strict terms of service, client confidentiality protocols, and statutory disclaimer structures.
